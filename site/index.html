Keep your MacBook's battery calm.

Calma limits how far your Mac charges, so the battery isn't sitting at 100% all day. Pick a limit, and the adapter powers your Mac while the battery rests.

Why limit charging?

Batteries age fastest when they're full and warm.

Lithium-ion cells wear faster at a high state of charge. A Mac that stays plugged in on a desk spends most of its life at 100%.

  • 1Hold it lower. Keeping the battery around 50–80% while plugged in puts less stress on the cells.
  • 2Keep it cool. Heat Guard pauses charging when the battery runs warm.
  • 3Charge fully when you need to. Full Charge gets you to 100% before a trip, then goes back to your limit.
Features

Everything included. Nothing locked.

Every feature is free. There's no Pro tier, no licence key and no account.

Charge Limit

Hold the battery anywhere from 20% to 100%. The adapter powers the Mac while the battery rests.

Drain to… & Auto Drain

Run from the battery while plugged in, down to a level you choose, or automatically whenever you lower the limit.

Drift Range

After reaching the limit, charging resumes only once the level drops a few percent below it. No constant 1% top-ups.

Heat Guard

Pause charging when the battery runs warmer than your threshold, with 5-minute windows so it doesn't flip back and forth.

Recalibrate

A guided 100% → 10% → 100% cycle with a one-hour hold that keeps the battery's percentage reading accurate.

Full Charge

One tap to 100% before a trip. Your limit comes back as soon as you unplug.

Sleep-aware

Pause on Sleep stops charging to 100% overnight, and Stay Awake to Limit keeps the Mac awake until the limit is reached.

Schedule

Set a limit, full charge, drain, pause or recalibrate daily, on weekdays, weekly, every two weeks or monthly, with a history log.

Power Flow

A live diagram of where the watts go between the adapter, battery and system.

MagSafe Light

Green at your limit, amber on the way there, optionally blinking while draining, or off. Only shown on Macs with MagSafe 3.

True Percentage & health

See the battery controller's raw percentage, cycle count, capacity and temperature.

CLI & Shortcuts

How it works

Small, auditable, and separated by privilege.

Charging is controlled by keys on the System Management Controller that only root can write. Calma keeps that part tiny and locked down.

Install and pick a limit

Drag Calma to Applications, open it, and choose a limit from the menu bar. 80% is a good start.

Approve the helper once

A small helper, calmad, runs as root. It's the only process that talks to the charging controller, and it only accepts a fixed set of commands from admins.

Forget about it

The helper checks your battery continuously and pauses or resumes charging. It keeps working across sleep and user switching.

Safety & privacy

Built to fail safe.

Calma controls real hardware, so it assumes things will go wrong and plans for it.

Safety

  • Normal charging comes back when the helper stops, the Mac shuts down, or you uninstall.
  • A heartbeat watchdog restores charging if the app goes quiet.
  • Only a short allowlist of documented keys can be written, and every write is logged locally.
  • Draining is refused below 20% or when temperature can't be read.
  • An Emergency Reset button and calma reset restore everything, and an SMC reset always does too.

Privacy

  • No analytics, crash reporting or account.
  • The only network request is an optional update check against GitHub Releases, and it's off by default.
  • Logs stay on your Mac.
  • Everything is open source, so you can read exactly what it does.
For power users

Script your battery.

The calma command talks to the same helper as the app. Use it in scripts, cron jobs, or Shortcuts with Run Shell Script.

$ calma status
$ calma limit 80          # hold the battery at 80%
$ calma drift on 5        # resume charging below 75%
$ calma heat on 35        # pause charging above 35 °C
$ calma fullcharge        # 100% once, back to limit on unplug
$ calma drain 60          # run from battery down to 60%
$ calma reset             # emergency reset

A note about macOS 27

macOS 27 firmware removed every publicly documented charge-control key. We confirmed this on an M4 MacBook Pro. Apple's replacement keys aren't documented and can't be read, so on macOS 27 Calma runs in monitoring mode: live telemetry, Power Flow and battery health work, but charge limiting is off.

We won't write guessed values to your charging hardware, or cycle the adapter on and off in a way that wears the battery. Until the new interface is understood, macOS's own System Settings → Battery → Charge Limit (80–100%) works, and Calma shows it. Help us research it →

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Calma really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no Pro tier, licence key, ads or account. Calma is licensed under GPL-3.0, so it stays open source.

Which charge limit should I use?

80% is a good everyday choice. If your Mac almost never leaves the desk, 50–60% is kinder still. Use Full Charge the evening before a day away from an outlet.

Why does it ask for an administrator password?

Only root can change charging behaviour. Calma installs one small helper, calmad, for that job. Everything else runs as your normal user.

macOS says the app can't be opened. What do I do?

Community builds aren't notarized yet. Right-click Calma in Applications and choose Open, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Calma.app. You can also build it from source.

Why did my Mac charge past the limit overnight?

The helper can't act while the Mac is fully asleep. Turn on Pause on Sleep, which stops charging before sleep, or Stay Awake to Limit.

My Mac stopped charging. Help!

Use Emergency Reset in the app or run calma reset. On Apple Silicon, a full shutdown followed by a 30-second wait always restores stock behaviour. The full recovery guide covers Intel Macs too.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes. The universal build supports Intel MacBooks from 2016 onwards on a best-effort basis. Hardware reports from Intel owners are very welcome.

Can I run it alongside another battery app?

Please don't. Two apps writing the same controller keys will fight each other. Remove the other app and its helper first.
